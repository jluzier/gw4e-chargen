# GAMMA WORLD 4E — Attribute Descriptions

Source: TSR 07514 - Gamma World, 4th Edition (1992), Chapter 2: Character Generation, "Attribute Scores" (p.16)

Tooltip/help-text ready descriptions for the seven primary attributes.

## PS — Physical Strength

Raw muscle power. Determines how much you can lift, push, or carry, and drives melee THAC and hand-weapon damage.

## DX — Dexterity

How nimble and accurate you are. Drives ranged THAC, Armor Class, Stealth, and combat initiative.

## CN — Constitution

How much punishment your body can take. Determines Hit Points and resistance to poison, radiation, and disease (Health).

## MS — Mental Strength

Willpower. The basis of mental combat — determines Mental Defense (MD) and factors into using mental mutations.

## IN — Intelligence

Logic and problem-solving. Governs your ability to figure out artifacts (Use Artifacts) and stay hidden (Remain Unseen).

## CH — Charisma

Social and psychological presence. Shapes how NPCs and other creatures react to you.

## SN — Senses

Overall sensory sharpness — sight, hearing, smell, etc. combined into one base score, the foundation for Perception.
